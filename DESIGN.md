# Nymrel Website Audit design contract

## Visual thesis

An editorial field report on warm paper: calm cedar typography, one terracotta signal, and the feeling of a trusted studio marking up the page beside you.

## Content plan

1. Outcome: Nymrel name, audited page, score, and a one-sentence verdict.
2. Priorities: the three fixes most likely to improve the page.
3. Detail: filterable checks with evidence and plain-language recommendations.
4. Follow-up: ask ChatGPT to explain the fixes, plus a concise methodology note.

## Interaction thesis

- The score ring and summary enter as one quiet reveal, establishing the result before detail.
- Filter controls move between all checks, needs-work checks, and passed checks without remounting the widget.
- Findings expand in place; the follow-up action hands the next question back to the conversation.

## Small-screen contract

The complete outcome and first priority must be visible at 375/390px without horizontal scrolling. Controls use 44px minimum tap targets, headings wrap naturally, and detail remains a document flow instead of collapsing into a dashboard grid.
